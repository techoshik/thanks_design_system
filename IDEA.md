A lightweight, accessible design system for building consistent “thank you” experiences across web and mobile products.

- Define reusable components for confirmation pages, gratitude messages, acknowledgments, donation receipts, and success states.
- Establish typography, color, spacing, iconography, and motion guidelines centered on warmth and clarity.
- Provide responsive, accessible variants with keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, and WCAG-compliant contrast.
- Document usage patterns, content guidelines, and do/don’t examples for designers and developers.
- Publish a versioned component library with a live showcase and adoption checklist.
